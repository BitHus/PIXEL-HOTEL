## 1. THE CORE: INN RENOWN (RN-1)

### 1.1 State and rules (new shared/src/renown.ts, pure)
- GameState.inn: InnState { rp: number; rankReached: number (1..10, never lowered); claimed: string[] (one-time RP keys such as 'book:25:foods', 'star:suite:3', 'bond:bram:2', 'project:hall:1'); later tasks add optional fields (regulars, board, staff, rent, projects, report, favourites). A normaliser `normalizeInn` fills defaults.
- Mess gets `rp: number` (computed when the guest leaves: rankFactor of the order + perfect + liked). RP is added when the player COLLECTS the coins (nothing automatic; away orders count the base 1).
- addRenown(state, key | null, amount, at): one function, ledger-style entry `renown` in the ledger (note = key), dedupes `key` against claimed, returns { state, rankUp?: number }. rankFromState(state, appeal, bookPct): highest r with rp >= RANKS[r].rp AND appeal >= RANKS[r].appeal AND bookPct >= RANKS[r].book; result = max(rankReached, that). rankProgress(...) returns three bars (rp, appeal, book) for the panel, with the plain hint of what is missing ("Add 12 appeal: place decor").
- Events: `guest_served`, `rank_up` go into the quest event counters (9i-1) so quests can ask for ranks.
- Old saves (migration): rankReached = 1; tavern level 2 -> 3, level 3 -> 6; rp = RANKS[rankReached].rp; claimed Book milestones that exist get their keys so nothing pays twice.

## 2. GUEST TASTES AND VARIETY (GT-1)

### 2.1 Rules (shared/src/tastes.ts, pure; data shared/data/tastes.json)
- Families come from data fields added to drinks.json and food.json (`family`: ale, stout, mead, wine, cider; roast, stew, pie, soup, bread, sweet...) and the existing rank and `tier` (aged, vintage). A Taste = { kind: 'family' | 'rank' | 'aged' | 'item'; value }. `rollTaste(rng, classId, menuFamilies)` weights toward families that exist in the game for that class (no taste is impossible to ever serve: test).
- chooseOrder(offers, tolerance, rng, taste?): if a liked offer is on the menu, in stock and accepted at its price, pick it with TASTE_PICK_CHANCE (0.7); else the old rule. The K2 `tasteOffers` (aged chance 0.25) stays and becomes the default taste for guests without one.
- Served liked: reaction 'big smile', `Mess.liked = true`, tip TASTE_TIP (10% of price, rounded, at least 1), gem chance times TASTE_GEM_MULT (2), +1 RP. Not liked: no penalty, ever.
- menuVariety(state) = distinct families among menu offers; toleranceWithVariety(t, v) = t * (1 + min(VARIETY_CAP 0.08, max(0, v - 2) * VARIETY_STEP 0.02)).
- Guest.taste is saved with the guest (optional field); the Taste is readable in the world only through a bubble line and a small icon on the table once the guest is seated (never a hidden stat the player cannot learn).
### 2.2 UI / art
Over a seated guest whose liked thing is on the menu: a small heart icon on the order bubble; the order bubble shows the item icon; a "likes ale" line when tapped. Menu panel: a VARIETY meter (families on the menu, +x% price tolerance) and per row a heart count "2 guests like this now". Art: heart/bubble variants, a happy sparkle on a liked serve (TA2).
### 2.3 Balance
Extra gold from tastes = tips only: expected +TASTE_TIP * P(liked served) ~ +3% of guest gold; variety up to +8% tolerance only moves accepted prices (the menu price steps stay data-bound). Tests: guest gold per hour with every taste always met and variety at cap <= the S3 hour * 1.12 and the share rules of 1.6 hold; gem share of the tavern tip stays inside maxSourceShare 0.4 (BALANCE_TARGETS); no taste needs an item the guest's class cannot order. Simulate adds a "tastes met" column.

## 4. THE ORDERS BOARD (BD-1, together with 9i questline)
### 4.1 Rules (shared/src/board.ts, shared/data/boardOrders.json)
- inn.board = { slots: Order[], sinceGuests: number, nextId, done: number }. Order = { id, template, from (a regular id or a class), item?, qty, progress, reward, text }.
- postOrder(state, rank, traitRng, at): only when a slot is free and sinceGuests >= ORDER_GAP_GUESTS (8). Template filter: unlocked by rank, the thing exists and is obtainable for this player (a reachability check shared with balance.ts: item is craftable/growable from what the player has unlocked, not from future content), not a duplicate of an open order. Never time-limited, never replaced by the clock.
- Kinds: `serve` (progress counts coins collected for that item class, fed by the tavern event), `bring` (Hand in: goods leave the bag by ledger 'board'), `craft` (a furniture or forged piece from the bag), `deed` (a counter from the 9i event stream: fish, kills, harvests, splices).
- Reward: `orderReward(template, qty)` = gold <= BOARD_GOLD_RATE (0.8) x sum(baseValue of the goods), plus RP (5, 10 for rare goods), gems 0 or 1 (rare+ only), sometimes an item; claim by tap (ledger 'board'). Decline = free, the slot refills after the next ORDER_GAP_GUESTS (no reward for declining, no cost).
- 9i-1 link: quests.json gets the same RewardSpec and claim code; board events (`order_done`, `rank_up`, `regular_bond`) are quest event types, so chapter 7 "The Inn grows" (data, TD4b) asks for them. A `from` regular's order raises their bond by 2 when done.
### 4.2 UI / art
Board = a wall item in the tavern (given at rank 2 and in the catalog for 40 gold); tap opens the BOARD panel (panelShell): order cards with a pinned-paper look (item icon, "Bram wants 3 roast turnips", reward icons, progress 1/3, Hand in / Decline), a pin dot on the info panel when an order can be handed in right now. Never a red badge pressure, just a quiet dot. Artist TA4: board prop (empty and with pinned papers by order count), paper card CSS (UI worker TU4), stamp "Done".
### 4.3 Balance
Board gold is its own bucket (5% to 20%). Tests: for every template and qty, reward gold + 30 * gems <= the guest revenue of the same goods (so the board is never better than serving); orders per hour <= slots * (guests per hour / 8) so gold per hour from the board <= BOARD_HOUR_CAP; every template item is obtainable at its rank; bring/craft rewards below the catalog price of what they ask (never a buy-craft-sell loop: catalog price * 3 vs reward 0.8 x baseValue).

## 15. RISKS
- Gold shares drift when supply grows with ranks: the rank-stage tests are the gate; fix by numbers (tolerance factors, class weights, seat caps), never by loosening.
- Rank gates feel like walls: the panel always shows the missing bar with a concrete action; points come from the thing the player already does; no gate is more than ~10 hours.
- Too many panels: renown, regulars, board, staff, projects share panelShell and one entry (Inn button in the right column: tabs Renown, Regulars, Board, Staff, Projects, Report); no new side buttons.
- Complexity of guests.ts: new traits live in tastes.ts, regulars.ts, guestClasses.ts and are only called from guests.ts (one line each); no new branch inside the step loop without a test.
- Closed inn + offline: a closed inn does not fill seats while away; the sign shows "Closed" in the Map card so nobody wonders why nothing happens.
