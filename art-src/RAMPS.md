# Colour ramps (Endesga 32, palette names from shared/src/palette.ts)

art-src/aseprite/ramps.aseprite is a 4 x 13 px sheet: one row per ramp, dark to light (left to right), one pixel per shade.
Shade with light from the top-left (lightest on the top and left edges, darkest on the bottom and right). The outline is always `outline`.

| Row | Ramp | Shades, dark to light |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | wood | walnut, oak, sand, cream |
| 2 | dark wood | outline, walnut, oak, tan |
| 3 | stone | night, dusk, slate, steel |
| 4 | metal | slate, steel, silver, white |
| 5 | brass | clay, orange, gold, butter |
| 6 | cloth red | crimson, red, rose, skin |
| 7 | cloth green | pine, forest, green, lime |
| 8 | leaf | forest, green, lime, butter |
| 9 | glass | steel, silver, cyan, white |
| 10 | clay | rust, clay, sand, skin |
| 11 | thatch | oak, tan, skin, cream |
| 12 | glow | orange, gold, butter, white |
| 13 | water | navy, sky, cyan, silver |
